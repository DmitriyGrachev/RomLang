# B2 dictionary stress audit

Read-only review of the 114 B2 dictionary entries as observed before the B2 author's corrections. I compared the displayed uppercase stress markers with the DOOM 3 (2021) orthographic entries hosted by dexonline; where the exact headword lacked a DOOM 3 entry, I used DOOM 2, DEX, or the same inflectional family. In the extracted DOOM text, the accented vowel appears separated by spaces, for example `prop u nere` = *propúnere*. The B2 author applied the correction list independently; this review did not edit `content/depth-B2.json`.

The multiword B2.d2102 had `a pune acCENtul`. `acCENtul` marks the noun correctly; the full phrase should show both content-word stresses: `a PUne acCENtul`, supported by [DOOM 2 pune](https://dexonline.ro/intrare/pune/46546/sinteza) (`púne`) and [DOOM accent](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/accent) (`accént`). This is an additional display consistency correction, yielding 22 edits in total.

## Coverage and limits

All 114 entries were compared, including 87 with directly extracted DOOM 3 headwords; the remaining 27 were checked against DOOM 2, DEX, and cognate or inflectional forms. DOOM 3 was not directly surfaced for those 27 on the queried pages, so this is not a claim of direct DOOM 3 confirmation for every entry. The corrections above are the clear mismatches. The 92 other entries had no detected stress change. This is a stress-only audit; meanings, examples, and task content were out of scope.
